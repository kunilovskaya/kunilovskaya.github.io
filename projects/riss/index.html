Reconfiguration and Internalization of Social Structure (RISS)

Data annotation: full parliamentary debates and party manifestos across five European countries (1980-present)

The project has developed an annotation scheme and is looking to contribute a large annotated dataset for multiple subprojects, exploring changes in social various segments of social structure as well as associated developments in political discourse.
